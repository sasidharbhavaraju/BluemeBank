Module 01 · The Autonomy Dividend Diagnostic

The Bridge
Diagnostic

Most Institutions are running invisible factories — reconciliation factories, interpretation factories, duplication factories, escalation factories. They have been normalised as the cost of doing business. This diagnostic names which factories yours is running — and calculates your Autonomy Dividend, a board-ready measure of how much autonomous capacity those factories are consuming today.

◈ Key Terms
AD
Autonomy Dividend
The headline metric. The percentage of an institution's consequential decisions that could safely operate autonomously today — given its actual data foundation, infrastructure, governance, and organisational readiness. Calculated as: min(DCI, RRV) × (DARR × DQIRR ÷ 100).
CIQ
Continuous Intelligence Quotient
The overall readiness score. Weighted toward the weakest dimension: 50% average of the four cardinal metrics plus 50% minimum score. A bank scoring 90/90/90/20 is not 72% ready — it is constrained by its weakest pillar.
Technical Autonomy Ceiling
min ( DCI , RRV )
The maximum AD an institution can achieve given its data and infrastructure constraints. Derived from the minimum of DCI and RRV — because you cannot outrun your bottleneck. Grounded in Liebig's Law of the Minimum: a system's output is constrained by its scarcest resource, not its average.
Operational Clearance Factor
DARR × DQIRR ÷ 100
The fraction of technically capable decisions that actually make it to autonomous execution without being blocked by governance or quality failure. Derived from reliability theory: independent failure modes compound rather than average. A 70% DARR and 60% DQIRR does not give 65% clearance — it gives 42%.
DCI
Data Convergence Index
The proportion of critical data entities — customer, account, counterparty, product — that share a common identity key resolvable across three or more core systems, with documented lineage. Three systems is the threshold: two sharing a key is often a point-to-point integration. Three signals genuine institutional coherence.
RRV
Regulatory Response Velocity
The average elapsed time between a new regulatory requirement being published and the institution having certified, lineage-traced data available to meet it. A proxy for technology infrastructure maturity — whether the data fabric can be adapted rather than rebuilt when requirements change.
DARR
Data Asset Reuse Ratio
The proportion of new use case data requirements met by reusing or extending existing certified data assets — rather than commissioning new ones. Measures whether the data programme is building shared institutional capability or repeatedly building the same data for different use cases.
DQIRR
Data Quality Issue Resolution Rate
The proportion of data quality issues raised by downstream consumers that receive a complete, traceable resolution response — root cause identified, upstream owner confirmed, fix timeline committed — within a defined service level. Measures whether accountability for data quality fixes is real or nominal.
