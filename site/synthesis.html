Glossary · Methodology
How This Is Calculated
① Section Scores
6 questions × score 1–4
Raw score ÷ 24 × 100 = percentage. Unknown answers score 1 — the most conservative assumption.
② CIQ Score
50% average + 50% minimum
Weighted toward the weakest dimension. A bank scoring 90/90/90/20 is not 72% ready — it is constrained by its weakest pillar.
③ Autonomy Dividend
min(DCI,RRV) × (DARR×DQIRR÷100)
Technical ceiling multiplied by operational clearance. Independent failure modes compound — they do not average.
◈ Projected Values
24 responses, not live data
Diagnostic estimate. Accurate to within the honesty of the responses given. Validate against operational data before board or regulatory submission.
Key Terms
AD — Autonomy Dividend
The headline metric. The percentage of an institution's consequential decisions that could safely operate autonomously today. Calculated as min(DCI, RRV) × (DARR × DQIRR ÷ 100).
CIQ — Continuous Intelligence Quotient
The overall readiness score. Weighted toward the weakest dimension: 50% average of four metrics plus 50% minimum score.
Technical Autonomy Ceiling
min(DCI, RRV). The maximum AD achievable given data and infrastructure constraints. Grounded in Liebig's Law: a system's output is constrained by its scarcest resource, not its average.
Operational Clearance Factor
DARR × DQIRR ÷ 100. The fraction of technically capable decisions that reach autonomous execution without governance or quality failure. Derived from reliability theory: independent failure modes compound.
DCI — Data Convergence Index
Proportion of critical entities — customer, account, counterparty, product — sharing a common identity key resolvable across three or more core systems. Three is the threshold: two signals a point-to-point integration, not institutional coherence.
RRV — Regulatory Response Velocity
Average elapsed time between a new regulatory requirement being published and the institution having certified, lineage-traced data available to meet it. A proxy for technology infrastructure maturity.
DARR — Data Asset Reuse Ratio
Proportion of new use case requirements met by reusing or extending existing certified data assets. Measures whether the programme builds shared institutional capability or repeatedly builds the same data for different use cases.
DQIRR — DQ Issue Resolution Rate
Proportion of data quality issues receiving a complete, traceable resolution response — root cause identified, upstream owner confirmed, fix timeline committed — within a defined service level.
